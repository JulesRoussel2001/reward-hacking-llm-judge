## Category D — master overstates available evidence

### D-1. "through Fable" / "Fable with its strongest available Anthropic model"

| | |
|---|---|
| paragraphs | P013 (confession), P044 (valuable context), P054 (objectionable context), P139 (Limitations) |
| claim | that the constructed rewrites were generated by "a stronger Anthropic model through Fable", and (P139) that generator and judge share a model family |
| evidence | `notes/atlas_c_v1_flags.md:9`, `notes/atlas_grid_v1_flags.md:8`, `notes/pilot1_analysis_prereg.md:323-324, 379-380` — all say only "authored by the assisting model", "authored in-thread, audited by owner" |
| category | **D** |

The repository records the *fact* of model-assisted authoring with human audit,
but never a provider, model name or version. `claude-fable-5` does appear in
`src/judge.py` (lines 80, 96) — but only as an entry in the harness's model
**capability registry** (`ADAPTIVE_ONLY_MODELS`, `EFFORT_LEVELS`), and it was
never used for any run (all 894 non-smoke calls are `claude-sonnet-5`). That
attests Fable as a known model family; it does **not** attest it as the
generator of the constructed prose.

**Recommended later action:** either supply the model identity from outside the
repository and record it in Appendix B, or soften the four sentences to the
provenance that is documented ("a stronger Anthropic model, audited by the
author" / "the assisting model"). P139's shared-model-family caveat is the most
exposed: it is a *limitation the master volunteers against itself*, so softening
it weakens a self-critique rather than a result — worth keeping if the identity
can be confirmed.
